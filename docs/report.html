Next Ride is ready to try

Built 2026-09-26. Phone web app that says which Disneyland Resort ride to do next, from live waits, your location and your list.

https://justinwi.github.io/disneyland-next-ride/

Put it on your phone

  • iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". It opens full screen like an app.
  • Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • Allow location when it asks. Without it, walks are measured from the Disneyland gate and the top of the screen says so.

Using it in the park

  1. Rides tab: tick the rides you want today, grouped by park and land. Each row shows the current wait. "Shows too" adds shows and walk-throughs.
  2. Next tab: the top card is the ride to do next, with its wait, the walk, and a line saying why. Two alternatives sit under it, then the rest of your list in order.
  3. Tap We rode it when you're done and the list re-ranks. Not now hides a ride for 45 minutes. Walk there opens Google Maps walking directions.
  4. The two chips at the top show how old the waits are and where it thinks you are. Tap the first to refresh and the second to re-find your location.
  5. More tab: walking pace (use Stroller with little kids), park-hop time, and which gate to measure from when location is off.

Your list stays on the phone. Done marks clear themselves each morning. Each phone keeps its own list.

How it picks

For each ride left on your list, it adds the walk to the wait you'll likely find when you get there, adding 20 minutes if the ride is in the other park. It then looks at the day's forecast for that ride. A ride whose line will be much longer later moves up, and one that will be shorter later moves down. A ride that closes before you could come back moves up. Rides that are down, closed, or that you can't reach before closing are listed separately with the reason.

Before the parks open, it shows which rides to hit first when the gates open, using each ride's usual wait at opening.

Update, 2026-09-26 evening: trip file and Lightning Lane

  • Trip file. Load a trip file in More, or open a private trip link. The list, priorities, avoid list, "unlock after" ladders stay on the phone and are never uploaded. Must-dos get a head start, avoided rides are never suggested, and locked rides wait for a thumbs up on the ride before them.
  • Trip days. Each trip day has a park, so the other park is never suggested. On a "decide" day it asks which park and suggests the one with more of your list left. Done marks carry across trip days.
  • Lightning tab. It says what to book with Multi Pass and when you're next allowed to book, which is after you scan in, then on tap-in or 2 hours after booking. It logs your bookings and shows a "use it now" card during each window. If a booked ride breaks down, it shows where to use the Multiple Experience pass. Standby picks that would make you miss a window are held back.
  • Ratings. After each ride, rate it 1 to 5 stars. Anything rated 4 or 5 shows under "Loved it? Ride again" with the current wait. One tap puts it back on today's list, and the same works on a later day.
  • Buy or skip, per day. A simulated day per park, from a week of real Lightning Lane history, gives a verdict, the minutes saved for the price, and a booking plan. Lightning Lane can be switched on or off per day, and on days without it the booking advice stays out of the way.

The full rules and the scoring are in design.html, section 10. A second adversarial review of this update reproduced 12 problems, 4 High and 8 Medium. All are fixed, re-tested and deployed. The two Low findings are recorded with their reasons in the design document.

With no signal, it keeps working from the last waits it saw. The age chip turns amber after 10 minutes and red after 30. There is never a spinner.

Update, 2026-09-27: the features you picked

  • Looks two or three rides ahead. Each pick has a "Then:" line naming the next one or two rides that make a sensible loop from it, with the walk and the wait you'll likely find. It re-plans after every ride, so the loop can change.
  • We're on our way. Outside the park, a button on the Next tab builds an arrival plan: which area to start in, the first three rides, and what to book with Lightning Lane as you walk in. Pick your travel time, 10 to 45 minutes. The plan clears itself once your phone is inside the park, or when you tap "We're here".
  • Reminders. They appear as a banner at the top of the screen, with a buzz on Android. iPhones don't let web apps vibrate. Each one fires once a day, and only while the app is open. They cover:
    • a Lightning Lane window opening in 15 minutes, or ending in 15;
    • "book your next Lightning Lane" the moment you're allowed;
    • a must-do closing within 45 minutes;
    • a ride on your list going down while you're heading to it, with the next best ride;
    • a down ride coming back up;
    • shows you've turned the bell on for, early enough to get a spot.
  • Morning briefing. Before noon on a trip day, a card gives the park and its closing time, anything on your list that's closed, the day's major shows, the Lightning Lane advice, and the "on our way" button.
  • Shows today. A fold-out list on the Next tab gives times and when to leave for a fireworks or parade spot. It also names which rides on your list usually get shorter during the show. The bell turns a show's reminder on or off.
  • Substitutes. When a must-do or high-priority ride is down or closed, it suggests up to two similar rides running in the same park.
  • Height check. In More, enter the shortest rider's height. Rides with a taller minimum drop out of the picks and the Lightning Lane advice, and are listed with the reason.
  • What to expect. Each pick has a one-line description, its height minimum, and a link to a preview video search.
  • Single Pass advice. For Radiator Springs Racers, the Lightning tab says whether to ride standby now, wait for its usual low point, or buy the Single Pass, with the minutes it saves.
  • Friday park chooser. On a "decide" day it compares the two parks: usual midday waits on that weekday, closing times, tonight's major shows, and how much of your list is left in each.
  • Battery saver. In More. Location gets coarser and waits refresh every 3 minutes instead of every minute.
  • No fixed midday break. The planner no longer assumes a 1 pm break. Take breaks whenever you like, and it re-plans from wherever you are afterwards.

Adversarial review

A separate reviewer attacked the deployed app and the engine with the real payloads, looking for ways it sends you to the wrong place, gets time wrong, fails silently, or loses your list. It reproduced 8 problems. All 8 are fixed, re-tested and deployed.

SeverityProblemNow
CriticalEvery evening, in the last 100 minutes or so before a park closed, the longest lines floated to the top labelled "last chance". A longer wait could make a ride score better.Fixed. A test checks that more wait, walk or hop never helps a ride's score.
HighOn the esplanade, one step north or south changed which park it thought you were in, adding or removing 20 minutes.Fixed. Outside both parks, walks to either park go through its gate.
HighRides that open early, which is common at rope drop, were hidden as "reopens 8 am".Fixed. A ride reported as running is ranked.
MediumOpening the app in the morning with no signal ranked last night's waits as if they were live.Fixed
MediumLate-evening claims like "Rise of the Resistance drops to 5".Fixed
MediumWith backup data, down or refurb rides said "closed for today".Fixed
MediumOne bad stored payload could freeze the app on "Loading" for good.Fixed
LowDone marks cleared at midnight even when the park was open until 1 am.Fixed. The day now turns over at 4 am.

The reviewer suspected these but couldn't reproduce them tonight. None is fixed yet, for the reason given.

  • Oogie Boogie Bash nights. After 6 pm on party nights, California Adventure is ticketed. If the feed lists those rides as operating, the app could send you there without a party ticket. Watch for this on a party night. It needs data from one to fix.
  • Morning feed rollover. If the feed still carries yesterday's hours before opening, the rope-drop preview won't appear. It needs a capture from before 8 am.
  • A frozen feed. If ThemeParks.wiki itself stops updating, the chip still says "just now", because it shows when the phone last fetched. This is rare, and the fix needs a baseline of normal update gaps first.
  • A ride closed for fireworks without a listed pause would say "closed for today" until it reopens. Not seen in the data yet.
  • Backup cache timing. Its files can be up to 5 minutes apart. The effect is small, and it only matters when the main source is down.
  • A rough first GPS fix used to pick the park. It's now guarded: a fix rougher than 250 m doesn't pick one.

Update 2026-10-04: thrills first, drop test, "We're hot"

  • Thrills first (Rides tab, on by default). Thrill rides become Must-do (⚡) and go first. Active rides move up one step, at most to High. Calm rides drop two steps and get a 10-minute handicap. Nothing leaves the list. Last-chance and "Try instead" alerts still follow the trip file's must-dos.
  • Drop test. Pirates' short dark drops gate Rise of the Resistance (which ends with a bigger dark drop), along with the Runaway Railway screen test. Test rides get a head start so they happen early. The card lists every ride a 👎 would skip.
  • Rise: pass or line. Once unlocked, a Next-screen card shows the Single Pass price, return time and standby, says which to do, and when the pass usually sells out.
  • 🥵 We're hot. For an hour, indoor and water rides go first, sunny queues drop back, and air-conditioned places nearby that you don't have to ride are listed.
  • Lightning Lane. Picks follow the thrill priorities. A ride still waiting on its test can be booked, but it counts half and says "only if … goes well first". It must also be bookable today in this park. The Lightning tab explains stacking, modify, the 1-hour window and why to arrive at opening.

Review 4 (one opus-high attacker on commit a90d6c7) found 10 problems: 0 Critical, 2 Medium-High, 4 Medium, 4 Low. Fixed: losing alerts for demoted must-dos; the first Lightning Lane pick being a ride still waiting on a test; gates in the other park; reloading the trip dropping Rise; the 👎 card under-stating what it skips; too-short rides counted as "waiting"; the drop-test line naming Rise after it was unticked; kiddie rides jumping past must-dos; and the cool-spot list (picked rides, non-AC walkthrough, duplicate, down spots). Accepted: the warm-up head start for Pal-A-Round Non-Swinging (it gates the swinging gondolas, so it should go first).

Not in this version

  • One list shared live across two phones. A private trip link sets both phones up once, but done marks and bookings stay per phone.
  • Actual ride lengths. Every ride counts as a flat few minutes on top of its wait.
  • The backup cache is refreshed by a GitHub schedule roughly every 5 minutes, sometimes later. GitHub pauses it after 60 days without a commit to the repo.
